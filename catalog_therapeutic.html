BiPAP Device (without backup rate)
HCPCS:
E0470
Common ICD-10 Codes:
G47.33, J96.10 (Respiratory failure), J44.1 (COPD)
INDICATIONS: OSA failing CPAP; restrictive lung disease; obesity hypoventilation; COPD with hypercapnia
REQUIRE: Failed CPAP trial OR complex sleep apnea OR hypoventilation with CO2 >45; sleep study; ABG if applicable
BiPAP Device (with backup rate)
HCPCS:
E0471
Common ICD-10 Codes:
J96.10, G47.30 (Central sleep apnea), G12.21 (ALS)
INDICATIONS: Central sleep apnea; Cheyne-Stokes; neuromuscular disease; chronic respiratory failure
REQUIRE: Sleep study showing central apneas OR respiratory failure with hypoventilation; backup rate medical necessity; ABG results
